## What changed during publication

The slide content, media and layout data in the public PowerPoint copies are unchanged. Only `docProps/core.xml` creator and last-modified-by values were cleared; originals are preserved in the private archive. The public PDF, supplied review images and photograph retain their original binary contents.

Cover, technology and student-journey PNGs were rendered from PDF pages 1, 6 and 7 for convenient viewing. They are previews of existing material, rather than new evidence of implementation.

The README files and explanatory documents were newly written for the portfolio with AI assistance, using the provided team materials and the owner's confirmed contribution scope. Metric definitions and implementation questions that expand the pitch are identified as portfolio explanations or proposed future work.
