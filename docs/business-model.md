# Business model and financial versions

Beacon proposes free student participation, with schools and councils as the recurring payers. Activity providers and prospective partners supply opportunities and potential in-kind support.

The concept separates student value from payment: students should be able to participate without a subscription charge. The standalone revenue graphic also states that Beacon would not sell student data or take a cut of venue transactions.

## Proposed revenue streams

| Stream | Price shown in the supplied material | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School subscription | AUD 10 per student per year; AUD 8,000 for an 800-student school | Modelled price; willingness to pay requires validation |
| Council catchment licence / co-funding | AUD 30,000 per catchment per year | Proposed arrangement |
| Build-year / public funding | Varies between financial snapshots below | Requested funding; no commitment evidenced |
| Microsoft licence credits and enablement | Value and credit assumptions vary by snapshot | Requested support; no approval evidenced |

## Reading the 19-page PDF snapshot

The displayed Year 1 cost components are staff AUD 55,000, licences AUD 13,856, platform AUD 60,000, other delivery AUD 25,000, rewards AUD 840 and contingency AUD 0. They sum to **AUD 154,696**. School and council contributions total AUD 70,000, leaving the displayed public funding gap of AUD 84,696.

The PDF's Year 5 values are scenario assumptions: total cost AUD 1,383,591, school revenue AUD 680,610, council revenue AUD 288,000 and public funding AUD 414,981. The claimed progression from about 45% to 70% earned-revenue coverage describes that model, not observed growth.

## Reading the standalone recurring-cost graphic

The graphic shows a recurring base of coordinator AUD 60,372, central licences AUD 17,152, platform maintenance AUD 12,000, legal/insurance AUD 4,000 and 10% contingency. After the AUD 30,000 council licence, it displays AUD 72,876 to cover.

It shows an established-school contribution of **AUD 6,237**: AUD 8,000 subscription less AUD 1,435 venue licences, AUD 168 rewards and AUD 160 contingency. The resulting 12-school threshold is arithmetic under those assumptions. It is not evidence of enrolled schools, signed contracts or an achieved break-even point. The same graphic lists 19 schools if the coordinator is full-time, without a full supporting calculation.

## How to reuse these figures

Use one snapshot at a time and identify it by artifact and page/slide. The funding ask from the 19-page PDF should not be combined with the standalone recurring-cost assumptions as if they came from a single reconciled model. Validate prices, scope, licensing credits, salaries and tax treatment before preparing a new budget.
